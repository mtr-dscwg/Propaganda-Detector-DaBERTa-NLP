# Results

### F. SI context length: 256 vs 512 tokens (seed 1, single run)

**Hypothesis** (from appendix A/B): Repetition fails end-to-end (FLC 0.066–0.087) because the
earlier occurrence of a repeated phrase falls outside stage 1's 256-token window. Doubling
the window should let the model see both occurrences.

Config: `--set tag=ctx512 --set si.max_length=512 --set si.batch_size=4` (batch halved to fit
8 GB VRAM; effective batch unchanged at 16 via grad_accum). Run folder: `runs/s1-ctx512`.
Stage 2 settings unchanged.

| Metric | 512-token SI (seed 1) | Baseline (6-seed mean) | Baseline (seed 13) | Baseline (seed 1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SI F1 | 0.4840 | 0.4840 ± 0.0124 | 0.4941 | — |
| TC micro-F1 (gold spans) | 0.6448 | 0.6655 ± 0.0057 | 0.6714 | 0.6590 |
| TC macro-F1 (gold spans) | 0.5257 | 0.5189 ± 0.0126 | 0.5083 | 0.5144 |
| FLC F1 (end-to-end) | 0.2252 | not yet measured | 0.2787 | 0.2564 |

**Findings**

1. **The hypothesis is not supported.** SI F1 landed exactly on the 6-seed baseline mean
   (0.4840), and Repetition's end-to-end F1 was unchanged at 0.066. Doubling stage 1's
   context neither helped nor hurt span identification.
2. **Cost without benefit:** 512 tokens roughly doubles training time per epoch and forced
   the batch size down. 256 stays the default.
3. **TC and FLC differences are not attributable to this change.** Stage 2's configuration was
   identical; only the seed and the spans handed to it differed. With no seed-averaged FLC
   baseline yet (seeds 1 and 13 differ by 0.023), the FLC drop to 0.2252 is within the range
   of what seed variation alone might produce.
4. **Macro up, micro down** (0.5257 / 0.6448 against 0.5189 / 0.6655): this run spread its
   predictions across rare techniques more, gaining on Flag-Waving (0.700), Slogans (0.714)
   and Whataboutism (0.364) while losing on the common classes. That is a class-balance
   effect, controlled by `tc.class_weight_power`, not by context length.
5. **Rows with n < 15 remain uninterpretable individually.** Thought-terminating Cliches shows
   a 1.00 FP rate on 6 gold spans.

**Next step for Repetition.** Measure the actual distance between a Repetition span and the
earlier occurrence of the same text, over the training set. If the median gap exceeds a window,
no context length will fix it, and the technique needs an article-level feature (e.g. a count of
how often the span's text occurs in the full article) rather than a longer window.

**Caveat:** single seed. To claim this properly, run `--set tag=ctx512` over 3+ seeds and
compare means.
